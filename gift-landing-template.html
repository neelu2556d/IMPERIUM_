Add to your home screen

It works like a native app — no app store download needed.

iOS iPhone & iPad

  1. Open Safari and go to the link above (or tap the CTA button).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with the arrow).
  3. Scroll the action sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  5. It appears on your home screen — just like a normal app.

AND Android

  1. Open Chrome and go to the link above (or tap the CTA button).
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install Imperium (or "Add to Home screen").
  4. Confirm with Install.
  5. It appears in your app drawer — works offline too.
