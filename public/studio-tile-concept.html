Where this is going next

Four moves, none in v1, each one small on the surface and large underneath. Marked clearly as coming, so nothing here overpromises.

Coming

Hook Doctor

Finds the exact second viewers drop from your retention curve, then rewrites that one moment so the next cut holds them.

Coming

Comment Miner

Reads your top comments and turns the questions people keep asking into next-video ideas, ranked by how often they come up.

Coming

Repurpose Engine

One long video becomes a week of shorts scripts, each cut around a single moment that already landed.

Coming

Voice-lock

Learns how you actually write, so every auto draft sounds like you, not like a model with a thesaurus.

·

What ships, in order

Highest-confidence code first · the AI magic once the spine is solid
  • v1.0Link library ships day one. Pure local · no key, no model, no network. Highest value per line in the whole set.
  • v1.0Connect + stats ride the existing YouTube connector. Fully shipped code, the single highest-confidence piece.
  • v1.0Manual video cards, every field typed by hand. Usable before any AI endpoint exists · stored in a proper studio_videos table, never the 512KB localStorage path.
  • v1.1Auto-package from a pasted or dropped script through one Anthropic proxy. The magic, with no transcription and no YouTube quota.
  • v1.1Vitality.report one stream · videos_published · so the tile lights up Vee.
  • shipRegistered as the first Arts District featured tile, installed through the existing importTile socket with its own /district page.
A note on what ships first
Link library, connect and stats, and hand-typed video cards are the v1 floor · every one usable before a single model call. Auto-package and the stats read follow, then the Studio registers as the first Arts District featured tile.
