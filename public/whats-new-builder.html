The tile builder, reborn.

You type a sentence. Vee builds you a real, living dashboard tile, for free, no Claude. Full screen, on the Vitality world. Here is where to tap and what to look for.

Try it
localhost:3000/app/create
  1. Land on the calm "What do you want to track?" Type a few words, or tap a seed.
  2. Watch the birth. The tile assembles on the bench: the ring sweeps, the number counts up, the bars rise, the world flares once.
  3. Talk to it in the Vee bar. Try dress it in iris cap it at 50 track it in ounces call it Morning Water. It actually reshapes, live.
  4. Scroll to "On your dashboard" to see it small, then click the tile to open it full screen, exactly like your real dashboard.
  5. Hit Add to dashboard. It lands on /app, empty and ready.
